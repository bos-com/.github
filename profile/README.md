# 🌐 Bugema Open Source Community (BOSC)

Welcome to the **Bugema Open Source Community (BOSC)** — a vibrant, inclusive, and collaborative innovation hub initiated by **Bugema University**.  
Founded by **Muwanga Erasto Kosea**, with support from **Open Elements** and **Support Care Uganda**, BOSC exists to solve real-world problems through **open-source technology and community collaboration**.

---

## 💡 Who We Are
**BOSC** brings together students, researchers, developers, and local communities to co-create impactful open-source projects that advance **sustainable development** in underserved and rural regions.  
We believe in the transformative power of **open knowledge**, **local innovation**, **inclusive technology**, and **community empowerment**.

---

## 🎯 Our Mission
**Empowering inclusive communities through open knowledge, local innovation, and technology.**

---

## ✅ Our Approach
- **Open Collaboration** — Everyone is welcome to contribute, learn, and innovate.  
- **Community Engagement** — We co-create relevant solutions with the people they’re meant to serve.  
- **Capacity Building** — Workshops, hackathons, and coding sessions nurture local digital talent.  
- **Sustainability First** — Every solution we build is scalable, affordable, and environmentally conscious.  

---

## 🌍 What We Do
- Develop open-source tools for crisis response, education, and digital inclusion.  
- Build partnerships between universities, communities, and innovators.  
- Promote open data and ethical technology practices in Africa.  

---

## 🤝 How to Get Involved
1. **Contribute** — Join one of our open projects on GitHub.  
2. **Collaborate** — Partner with BOSC on digital transformation initiatives.  
3. **Mentor or Learn** — Take part in our workshops, talks, and innovation challenges.
